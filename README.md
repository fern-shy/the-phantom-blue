# the phantom blue

An ornate iron gate on black, divided into a 3×3 grid of nine zones. Loops keep running once loaded; hover and click decide who is heard.

**Run it:** double-click `index.html` (Safari or Chrome). Fully offline, single file, no install. On the entrance: *load* (first nine audio files fill the grid) then *enter*.

- **Load** on the entrance, or **drop** / **click** an empty zone after entering. Several files fill empty zones in name order. double-click clears.
- **Hover** swells that zone. **Click** a loaded zone to pin it as the main voice — it stays up. Hover then blends other zones on top of it. Click the main again to unpin, or click another zone to move the pin (the old main releases).
- Release defaults to its maximum of 20 seconds. A sounding zone **inverts its own slice of the gate**; crossing a loaded zone snaps the inversion instantly.
- **control** folds the four sliders away; click it and they splay out beside it. **master / tone** (low-pass) to tuck it under the band. **drift** — click to wander slowly when idle, or click-and-drag away from the button to set the cycle speed (farther = faster; the far end is nearly a blur). **space** = hush (instant mute/unmute). **f** = fullscreen.
- Files: WAV/MP3/M4A/FLAC work everywhere; AIFF only in Safari. For seamless loops use WAV and a different (odd) length per zone so they drift against each other.
- Files are not remembered between reloads (re-drop them).
